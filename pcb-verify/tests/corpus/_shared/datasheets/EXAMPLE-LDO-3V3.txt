--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 5  Application Information
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A 1 uF ceramic capacitor is required at VIN and a 1 uF ceramic capacitor is required
at VOUT. Place both capacitors within 5 mm of the device pins. X5R or X7R dielectric
is recommended; account for DC bias derating when selecting the capacitor case size.

The EN pin has no internal pull-up or pull-down. An external resistor is required to
define the state of EN before firmware runs.
