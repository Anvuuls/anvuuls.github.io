EXAMPLE CONNECTOR CORPORATION
EXAMPLE-CONN-2P  --  2-position 2.54 mm pitch vertical header
Document EX-CN-0002  Revision 1.0

This is a FICTIONAL part used by the pcb-verify test corpus.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 1  Ratings
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current rating per contact ........... 3 A
Voltage rating ....................... 250 V AC
Mating cycles ........................ 30 minimum
Operating temperature ................ -40 C to 105 C

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 2  Pin Assignment
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Table 2-1  Contact numbering

Contact 1 is marked on the moulding by a triangular indicator and is the contact
nearest the chamfered corner of the housing.

Pin   Name    Type      Description
1     P1      passive   Contact 1, nearest the chamfered corner marking.
2     P2      passive   Contact 2.

The header is unpolarised and unkeyed. Reverse insertion of a mating cable is
mechanically possible, so the board design must tolerate reversed connection or
provide keying externally.
